## No Exchange Licensing, No Extra Fees

A business that shows market data to its users usually has to sign agreements with each exchange, pay monthly exchange fees, and pay again for every user who sees the data. Those costs grow with every user you add, and the paperwork comes before you can launch.

The Commercial Plan removes all of it. Real-time prices, delayed exchange data, historical data, and tick-level data all come under one license, at one price. You sign nothing with the exchanges, you pay no exchange fees, and you pay nothing more as your user base grows — so your data costs stay the same whether your product has ten users or ten thousand.

## What You Get with the Commercial Plan

### Data

- **Real-Time Stock Prices**: Real-time derived prices that closely track exchange prices, with last price, open, close, volume, and pre-market and post-market volume.
- **Real-Time Options Prices**: Real-time derived options prices, with implied volatility and the greeks — delta, gamma, theta, and vega.
- **WebSocket Streaming**: Stream real-time stock and options prices over WebSocket, or request them over the REST API.
- **Tick-Level Market Data**: Market data down to the individual tick.
- **15-Minute Delayed CBOE Data**: Exchange data from CBOE on a 15-minute delay.
- **End-of-Day and Historical Data**: End-of-day and historical data for stocks and options, for backtesting, analytics, and historical charts.
- **US Fundamentals**: Fundamentals data for US companies.

### Rights

- **Commercial Use and Display Rights**: A business-wide commercial license. Use the data across your business and display it to the users of your app, dashboard, or product.
- **No Exchange Licensing**: No exchange agreements to sign and no exchange paperwork.
- **No Exchange Fees or Per-User Fees**: One price, however many users see the data.
- **No IP Limit**: Call the API from as many servers and IP addresses as your product needs.

### Tools and Limits

- **AI Assistant Access**: Connect Claude, ChatGPT, and other AI assistants to the data through an MCP server.
- **Rate Limits**: 2,000 API calls per minute, 1 request per minute for bulk downloads and paging, and 3 WebSocket connections.
