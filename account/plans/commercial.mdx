## What You Get with the Commercial Plan

- **Commercial Use and Display Rights**: A business-wide commercial license. Use the data in your business and display it to the users of your app, dashboard, or product.
- **No Exchange Fees or Paperwork**: No exchange agreements to sign and no exchange fees to pay. See [About the Real-Time Prices](#about-the-real-time-prices) below.
- **Real-Time Stock Prices**: Real-time derived prices for stocks, with last price, open, close, volume, and pre-market and post-market volume.
- **Real-Time Options Prices**: Real-time derived options prices, with implied volatility and the greeks — delta, gamma, theta, and vega.
- **WebSocket Streaming**: Stream real-time stock and options prices over WebSocket, or request them over the REST API.
- **15-Minute Delayed CBOE Data**: Exchange data from CBOE on a 15-minute delay.
- **End-of-Day and Historical Data**: End-of-day and historical data for stocks and options, for backtesting, analytics, and historical charts.
- **Tick-Level Market Data**: Market data down to the individual tick.
- **US Fundamentals**: Fundamentals data for US companies.
- **AI Assistant Access**: Connect Claude, ChatGPT, and other AI assistants to the data through an MCP server.
- **Rate Limits**: 2,000 API calls per minute, 1 request per minute for bulk downloads and paging, and 3 WebSocket connections.

## About the Real-Time Prices

The real-time stock and options prices on the Commercial Plan are **derived prices**, not exchange quotes. They are calculated to track exchange prices closely, but they are not exchange data, so they carry no exchange licensing requirements. That is what lets the plan include commercial display rights with no exchange fees and no exchange paperwork.

Exchange-sourced data on the Commercial Plan comes as **15-minute delayed CBOE data** and as **end-of-day and historical data**. If your product needs real-time exchange data, such as live bid/ask quotes from the exchanges, that requires exchange licensing, which the Commercial Plan does not include. [Contact our sales team](https://www.marketdata.app/contact/) to discuss it.

## Who the Commercial Plan Is For

The Commercial Plan is for businesses bringing a product to market on top of market data. Common use cases include:

- **Stock and options apps** that display data to paying users
- **Financial dashboards** distributed to clients or employees
- **Research products and newsletters** that publish data-driven content
- **Charting and analytics tools** built on real-time and historical data
- **Internal business analytics** at firms that need market data for internal use

### Not Suitable for Trading

The Commercial Plan is **not suitable for trading applications** or any product where users make execution decisions based on the data. Its real-time prices are derived prices — useful for display, charting, and informational purposes — but they are not live bid/ask quotes from the exchanges, and they should not be used to determine trade prices, route orders, or trigger execution logic. Trading platforms and order-routing tools require real-time exchange data, which means direct exchange licensing. [Contact our sales team](https://www.marketdata.app/contact/) to discuss licensing options for trading use cases.

## Redistribution Rules

The Commercial Plan permits you to **display data to the users of your own product**, but does **not** permit you to redistribute the raw data itself. In other words:

- ✅ You can build an app, dashboard, or product that displays the data to your users.
- ✅ You can build SaaS and fintech products on top of the data.
- ❌ You cannot resell or redistribute the raw data.
- ❌ You cannot offer the data as a standalone data service.
- ❌ You cannot white-label the raw data or license it to third parties.

If your business needs to resell data or offer a market data API to other businesses, [contact our sales team](https://www.marketdata.app/contact/) to discuss your use case.
